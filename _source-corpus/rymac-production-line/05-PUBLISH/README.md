# 05-PUBLISH

Stages 8 and 9. The video becomes a package, a publish kit, and a blog post.

- The community package: drop post, strategy files that each end in a runnable
  prompt, a term sheet, a member-builds file, and the zip.
- The YouTube publish kit: your locked title, 2 alternates, the description
  built on YOUR swipe, real chapter timestamps computed off the render, and 2
  thumbnails rendered through the same pipeline as the video.
- The blog post: every live video gets one behind it, on your own site,
  claiming its own keyword row.

Skills that run here: `rymac-package-a-video-for-your-community`, then
`rymac-write-an-seo-blog-post`. Both run under
`rymac-write-in-the-owners-voice`.
