# The Errand Boy Test

**From:** Stop Being the Errand Boy Between AI Agents
**Watch it:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En4IeyJ8JoI

---

## The 30 second version

If you are the thing carrying information between your AI tools, you do not have a
system. You have a job.

That is the whole test. Everything below is just how to run it honestly on yourself.

---

## What it actually looks like

You explain the same thing to agent 1.

Then you explain it again to agent 2.

Then agent 3 asks you who the client is.

Nothing ever syncs. So you become the errand boy for software you paid for.

Here is the part that matters: **that is not a skill problem and it is not your
fault.** Every one of those tools starts with a blank memory. None of them can see
what the other one just did. The only thing holding the job together is you,
retyping the same context over and over.

You did not build a bad system. You became the missing piece of a system that was
never finished.

---

## The 4 questions

Answer these out loud. Do not soften them.

**1. How many times this week did you paste the same background into a different
tool?** If the answer is more than twice, you are the message bus.

**2. If you disappeared for 3 days, would any of it keep working?** Not "would it
be fine." Would it RUN. If the answer is no, the tools are not doing the job, you
are.

**3. Can you point at the 1 place that says what your business is?** One file. One
page. If your answer is "it's kind of spread across a few places," then your AI is
guessing every single session and so are you.

**4. When something comes back wrong, do you know WHY?** If the honest answer is
"no, I just try again with a better prompt," you cannot fix it, you can only
gamble.

---

## What a pass looks like

A pass is not "I have fewer tools." A pass is: **the work can find its own
context.**

You ask a question. The thing doing the work goes and reads. It comes back with an
answer and tells you where it got it. You never once explained who the client is.

That is 1 worker with a map, and it is boring on purpose. Boring is the point.
Boring does not drift.

---

## The mistake almost everybody makes here

They run this test, feel the pain, and their first move is to add ANOTHER tool that
promises to connect the other tools.

That is the same disease in a new coat. You are not short a tool. You are short an
organized place for the work to read from.

Fix the filing before you buy anything else.

---

## Run this prompt

Paste this into Claude, ChatGPT, or whatever you use, then answer its questions
honestly. It is going to be uncomfortable and that is how you know it is working.

```
I want you to run an honest diagnostic on how I am currently using AI in my
business. Do not encourage me and do not soften your read.

Here is my situation:
- What my business does: [DESCRIBE IT IN 2 SENTENCES]
- The AI tools or agents I currently use: [LIST THEM]
- What I use each one for: [ONE LINE EACH]
- The last 3 times AI work came back wrong: [DESCRIBE BRIEFLY]

Ask me these 4 questions one at a time, and wait for my answer before moving on:

1. How many times in the last 7 days did I paste the same background context into
   more than one tool?
2. If I stepped away for 3 days, which of these would keep running without me?
3. Where is the single place that describes what my business is and what I sell?
4. When something comes back wrong, do I know why, or do I just retry?

After all 4 answers, tell me plainly:

A. Am I the thing carrying information between my tools? Yes or no, then why.
B. Which of my tools is genuinely earning its place, and which one exists only
   because I am manually feeding it.
C. The single smallest change that would stop me being the message bus. Not a new
   tool. A change to how my information is organized.
D. What that change would look like as actual folders and files on my computer.
   Give me the exact names.

Write it at a 9th grade reading level. No jargon. If you use a technical word,
define it in the same sentence.
```
