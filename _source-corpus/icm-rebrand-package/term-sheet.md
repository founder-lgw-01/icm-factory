# Term Sheet

Every term from the video, in plain language, 1 line each.

- **ICM (Jake Van Clief's folder system)** - running AI off plain folders and text files: a map at the top, instructions inside each folder, numbers on the folders so work happens in order.
- **The walk-away test** - Jake's rule: if you can't ask it to do something and walk away, you're doing it wrong.
- **Claude Code** - the AI that did the rebuild. It reads your folders and files, then does the work.
- **The map** - the file at the top that says where everything lives and what should never be touched.
- **The ledger / the list** - the inventory file that already knew which 8 lessons wore the dead brand before the prompt existed.
- **The brand law file** - the file that spells out the look: colors, font, ending card, what's banned.
- **The notes** - the file holding the stuff you'd otherwise re-explain every session, like the cloud setup.
- **The prompt** - the 1 message describing the outcome: rebrand all 8, cut my old closes, new end card, do not make me reshoot anything.
- **The dead brand** - the old look the 8 lessons were still wearing. Rebrands kill brands; the content survives.
- **Reshoot** - re-recording yourself on camera or mic. The whole point was 0 of these.
- **The close (of a video)** - the ending pitch. The old ones pitched things that no longer exist, so they got cut, not re-said.
- **Amazon's computers (the cloud)** - rented computing that rebuilt the videos: about 30 seconds and about 6 cents each, instead of 1 hour each on my laptop.
- **The approval email** - Amazon's 2:09 PM yes to my request for more computers, mid-run. 1 copy and paste and 7 of the 8 moved to the cloud.
- **The word check** - verifying every cut word by word against what's actually in the finished file. It caught 1 leftover word before I ever saw it.
- **The 90 second preview** - the short approval clip I watched before anything went live. I typed 1 word: approved.
- **The receipts / the terminal** - the actual window from that day, every step written down, the thing I scroll through in the video.
- **The artifact** - the results page that was waiting on my screen when I walked back in.
- **1 thing, 1 time** - the starting rule: not a whole system in 1 day. Ask for 1 thing, walk away, learn, continue on.
