This folder holds YOUR published writing. 2 or 3 real pieces you actually
posted and liked. An email, a community post, a video description.

Why it exists: every writing skill in this kit calibrates on these samples
before it writes a word under your name. No samples means the AI guesses at
how you sound, and it will guess wrong in the most generic way possible.

Paste each piece into its own .md file. Real ones, not ones you wish you had
written.
