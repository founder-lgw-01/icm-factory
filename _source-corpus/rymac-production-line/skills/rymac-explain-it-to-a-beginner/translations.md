# The sandwich dictionary

## The format, and why it is not just deleting the word

> You may hear that called **<their word>**. It simply means **<his plain version>**.

Deleting the fancy word is the wrong fix. It kills the search term, and it leaves
the reader defenceless the next time a guru says it at them.

The sandwich does 3 jobs at once:

1. The keyword stays on the page, so the ranking survives.
2. The reader gets armed for when somebody uses it on them.
3. It puts the owner in the translator seat, which is the entire moat.

His own letter is the model:

> "Most of what people call **agent orchestration** is just poor filing."

**Never write a fancy word in his voice as though he owns it.** He does not own
those words. He does not talk that way.

The ones that keep slipping in are `context engineering`, `orchestration`,
`agentic`, `sequential`, `concurrency`, `persistent memory`, and `reconciling`.

On 2026-08-16 a sweep of 12 blog posts found 86 of them.

**The deeper bank is the `term-sheet.md` file** inside
`community/skool/packages/icm-desk/`, `errand-boy/`, and `folder-system-41-days/`.
Check there before you invent a definition. He has already explained all of it.

## Never swept, 3 exemptions

**1. Interpretable Context Methodology.** A proper noun, the name of Jake Van
Clief and David McDermott's paper. It stays, always followed by ICM, always with
the credit and the link `https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16021`. That is attribution
and it is the search term.

**2. The real file names.** `CLAUDE.md` and `Context.md` are what the files are
actually called. Say the name, then say what it is: "a file called `CLAUDE.md`,
which is the card taped to the desk."

**3. Product and tool names.** These are Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude Code,
Notepad, and your community platform. Those are names and they are not jargon.

## Words that are banned outright, no sandwich

These are not fancy words to translate. They are wrong, and they do not go on any
surface.

- **"Naming the folders."** It describes the motion, not the result. Say what the
  buyer gets: 1 place their business runs from.
- **"Cohort."** Jake runs one. PaidOnDeploy runs one. Never used as a villain word.
- Never use **"sold"** or **"selling"** as the verb. The verb is "closed". The
  only survivor is the doctrine name "Sell First, Build Second".
- **Any token saving percentage.** He did not measure it. 20%, 40%, 80%, all out.
  > "I'm not going to repeat a number I didn't measure myself."
- **State machine, array, pipeline, schema, commit, merge, diff, branch** used
  straight. If a salesperson would not say it on a live call, it does not ship.
