# It Works. Then It Rots.

**From:** Stop Being the Errand Boy Between AI Agents
**Watch it:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En4IeyJ8JoI

---

## The 30 second version

Nobody warns you about this part: it is not that the system does not work. It works
for about 3 weeks. Then it quietly stops being true, and nothing tells you.

Rot is not a bug in your system. Rot is what happens to every system. The only
question is whether you have something that catches it.

---

## A real one, in his own words

I read 90 posts in the room where people who believe in this stuff go when it
breaks. This is one agency owner, in order, across his own thread:

"it's an agency style business"

"For the first few weeks it was working really great"

"But then I started to notice a deterioration"

"Previous templates were subtly changing over time"

"I feel like I'm correcting things more than I should be"

"and my productivity is getting impacted"

**"the integrity of my framework has deteriorated"**

"worried about having to rework my entire repo"

Read that again. He built it himself. It worked. Now he is scared to touch his own
system.

That is not a beginner. That is somebody who did it right and got bitten by the
thing nobody sells you a warning about.

---

## Mine rots too

I am not up here telling you I built a perfect system.

The first file anybody reads in my whole business still said I sold a brand I had
killed weeks earlier.

Nothing broke. Nothing turned red. No error. It just quietly told every new session
the wrong thing about my own business, and it kept doing it until I went looking.

**That is what makes rot dangerous.** A broken thing screams. A rotted thing keeps
answering confidently with old information.

---

## The Camry rule

Toyota Camrys are known to last forever. But you do have to maintain them. Check
the fluids, change the oil, air up the tires.

Or it rots.

Nobody thinks a Camry is badly built because it needs oil. Same deal here. A folder
system that needs a check is not a broken folder system. It is a normal one.

The people who get burned are the ones who were sold "set it up once and you are
done," because when it drifts they think they failed.

---

## How to catch it

The trick is not discipline. You will not remember to audit. The trick is having
something that reads your own files back to you and tells you where they disagree
with reality.

I built a check and named it after the feeling that made me build it: **am I
crazy?** When I get that itch that something is off but I cannot name it, the work
stops and it runs.

It found my dead brand in 1 pass and told me in plain English.

---

## The 4 places rot shows up first

**1. What you sell.** Prices, packages, and offers change faster than your files do.

**2. Who you are.** Brand names, positioning, the thing you say you do. This is the
one that got me.

**3. Dead links to dead things.** A file that points at a folder you renamed 3
weeks ago.

**4. Two files that disagree.** The oldest and quietest failure. Both look fine
alone.

---

## Run this prompt

Run this against your own files. Do it the first time today, and then any time you
get that "something is off" feeling.

```
I want you to audit my business files for rot: places where what is written down
has quietly stopped being true.

Read these files/folders: [PASTE THE FILES, OR POINT YOUR AI AT THE FOLDER]

Here is what is ACTUALLY true right now, today:
- What I sell and what it costs: [CURRENT TRUTH]
- What my business is called and how I describe it: [CURRENT TRUTH]
- Anything I stopped doing or killed off recently: [LIST IT]
- Anything I started recently that may not be written down yet: [LIST IT]

Now find the drift. Check these 4 things specifically:

1. WHAT I SELL: anywhere the files describe an offer, price, or package that does
   not match what I just told you.
2. WHO I AM: anywhere the files still use an old name, old positioning, or a brand
   or product I have killed.
3. DEAD POINTERS: anywhere a file refers to another file, folder, or link that does
   not exist anymore.
4. CONTRADICTIONS: any 2 files that say different things about the same fact. Show
   me both, side by side.

For every single thing you find, give me:
- The exact file and the exact line
- What it says now
- What it should say
- How bad it is: is this actively telling people the wrong thing, or is it just
  untidy

Then rank the fixes. Most damaging first. If a file is clean, say so plainly
instead of inventing a problem to look useful.

Write it at a 9th grade reading level.
```
