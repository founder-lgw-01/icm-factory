# Write To Who It Is FOR, Never Indict A Genre

This one I caught live on camera and killed, and it is the most useful 30
seconds in the whole video.

## The headline I almost shipped

The research said my page had 2 audiences: people who can build but cannot
sell, and people who can sell but cannot build. So the first headline came
back as:

> Builders can't sell. Closers can't build. Fix your half.

It is punchy. It names both. And it is wrong, because it makes a blanket
statement about an entire group of people, and half of them are reading it
thinking "no I can't, who is this guy?"

**Do not describe your reader in a way that insults everyone like them.** You
sound like an asshole, and nobody buys from that.

## The fix is 1 word

Change it from ABOUT to FOR.

> **For** builders who can't sell. **For** closers who can't build.

Same 2 audiences. Same self-selection. Zero insult. The person it does not
describe reads it, thinks "not me," and moves on without being told what
they are.

That line went in the eyebrow, which is the small line above the headline.
Which frees up the headline to do its actual job.

## What goes in the headline then

The dream outcome, with a timeframe, using a named thing.

> Learn To Build, Market, & Close In 3 Hours. Free.

Dream outcome: build, market and close. Timeframe: 3 hours. Named thing: the
free course. Under 10 words.

**And the number has to be real.** I measured the actual runtime of every
video in my library before I put a number on the page. It came out at 2.5
hours and I put 2 on the page. Under-promise, because the timeframe is the
most checkable claim you have and a burned market will check it.

## The confidence axis nobody talks about

Here is what the research surfaced that changed my angle.

Builders are terrified of selling, and they are slowly gaining confidence.
Closers are the opposite, short on confidence about building.

Same fear, opposite ends. Which means the promise is not "become a different
person." It is "get good at the half that scares you."

That matters, because these people do not want to be turned into a
salesperson. One of the quotes that came back was somebody saying they want
the burden on the system, not on their personality.

Sell them the system. Never sell them a personality transplant.

## The support line

Under the headline, 1 line that lets them enter wherever they are:

> Watch it all or the part you're stuck on.

That is doing real work. A builder and a closer need different starting
points, and this tells both of them they are not required to start at video 1
and slog.

## Run this prompt

```
Write the hero section for my offer.

My offer: [WHAT YOU SELL AND THE RESULT IT GETS]
Who it is for: [THE AUDIENCE OR AUDIENCES. If there are 2 different kinds
of buyer, say both.]
The real, measured proof I can use: [ANY NUMBER YOU CAN ACTUALLY DEFEND.
Runtime, count, timeframe. If you have none, say so and do not invent one.]

Rules, follow all of them:

1. The eyebrow names who it is FOR. Never write a line that makes a blanket
   claim about a group of people. "For X who struggle with Y" not "X can't
   do Y."
2. The headline is the dream outcome, with a timeframe, using a named thing.
   Under 10 words.
3. Every number must be one I gave you above. If I did not give you one, do
   not put one in, and tell me which number would make this stronger so I
   can go measure it.
4. One support line under the headline that lets different buyers enter at
   different points.
5. Give me 5 headline options, then tell me which you would ship and why.
6. Then check your own work: does any line here insult the reader or anyone
   like them? Rewrite anything that does.
```

Point 6 is the one that saved my page. Make the AI audit its own output for
insult, because it will not catch it on the way out.
