# Nobody Argues With Their Own Math

Yo! It's RyMac. This is the sales lesson hiding at the end of the video, and
it is the best one I know.

## The story

A company hired me to fix a sales cycle that took 64 days to close. I got it
down to 1 call. Not with a demo. Not with a deck. With an Excel sheet.

On the call I would ask THEM for the numbers. Their leads. Their close rate.
Their profit per sale. The sheet added it up in front of them. Then I would
ask if they saw it working, and they always said the same thing: "Well, no,
I mean, I see it working, but what's the cost?"

Boom. 1 hidden row left: the cost. They had already agreed to every number
above it, because every number was theirs.

## The line that runs it

25 years of closing compresses into 1 line:

**Never tell them anything you can get them to say themselves.**

They will not believe what you say. They will believe everything they say.

And notice: I ran this exact technique on you in the video. I never told you
what YOUR stack costs. I told you mine, then pointed you at a calculator
that makes you say your own number. That calculator is my best closer, and
it never says a word.

## The move for your business

Stop telling people what they are losing. Build the thing that lets them
tell you. It does not have to be software on day 1. A shared spreadsheet on
a call works. A 5-question worksheet works. The mechanics that matter:

1. Every input is THEIR number, asked out loud or typed by them.
2. The math runs in front of them, visibly, step by step.
3. The one number you want them to feel comes LAST, after they have agreed
   to everything that produces it.
4. Then you shut up and let their number do the closing.
