# Why 6 Robots Made It Worse

Yo! It's RyMac. This is the road I went down first, and the road most of the
industry went down first. If you are already building a team of AI agents,
read this before you build the next one.

## The obvious fix that is not a fix

The desk is too small. So: get more desks.

Build a team of AI agents. One to research, one to write, one to check the
work, one to manage the other 3. It sounds like scaling. It sounds like what
a real business would do.

Now you have 6 desks. 6 piles. **And not one of them can see anybody
else's.**

So every single one has to be told everything from scratch, and your actual
job becomes managing handoffs between 6 things that are blind to each other.

Look at where that leaves you. Right there. In the middle. Walking between
the desks.

## That was me

That was my whole setup. Nobody sold me a multi agent framework. I just built
one by accident, one AI at a time, because each one on its own was genuinely
useful.

I had one doing my copy. One doing code. One doing research. And I was the
messenger boy between my own robots, walking back and forth all day, copying
what this one said, carrying it over to that one, explaining the whole
situation again from scratch because that one had no idea what just happened,
then carrying the answer back.

Every single time.

And the wild part is I thought that was the job.

**The AI was not broken. My structure was.**

## The actual fix

You do not need 6 desks. **You need one desk that does not fill up.**

One desk. One card. One folder at a time, in order, with you looking at each
one before it moves on.

This is also why those setups look unbelievable in a demo and fall apart the
second you point them at your real work. A demo has no history. Your business
is nothing but history.

## Run this prompt

Paste this into your AI along with this file:

```
I want to find out if I have accidentally built a relay.

Here is every AI tool, assistant, custom GPT, or agent I currently use, and
what each one does for me:

[LIST THEM]

For each one, tell me honestly:
1. What I have to re-explain to it every time I open it. That re-explaining
   is the cost.
2. Whether it could read the same folder as the others instead of being its
   own island.
3. Where I am acting as the messenger, carrying output from one to another
   by hand.

Then collapse it. Show me what ONE setup would look like: what the card says,
what the drawers are, and what I would lose by consolidating. If some of them
genuinely should stay separate, say which and why, and do not consolidate for
the sake of a tidy answer.
```

If the answer is that you are the messenger, that is not a workflow. That is
a job you gave yourself.
