# Rebrand Without Reshooting

From the video: "1 Prompt Rebuilt My Entire Content System (Jake Van Clief ICM Folder System)"

## The numbers from the day

- 8 videos, about 62 minutes of finished training, moved to a new brand
- My recording time: 0 minutes. My original voice, untouched
- Every old ending cut out clean
- About 30 seconds a video to rebuild on Amazon's computers, about 6 cents each, about 45 cents for the whole batch
- New lead magnets and drop posts built alongside them, right the first time

The old way to do this is a week of re-recording, re-editing, and re-uploading. On my dated 8 GB Lenovo laptop, probably 2 weeks. The reason I didn't have to: my voice and my visuals were stored as separate ingredients, not baked into 1 finished file. Change the look, keep the voice, rebuild.

## What actually changes in a rebrand

Almost nothing you'd reshoot for. Walk any old piece of content and sort it:

1. **The skin.** Colors, fonts, logos, the ending card. All swappable without touching your voice.
2. **The dead phrases.** The old brand's name, the old tagline, the old pitch at the end. These get CUT, not re-said. My old endings pitched things that don't exist anymore, so they just ended earlier.
3. **The teaching itself.** The reason people watched. This is usually 95% of the runtime and it doesn't change at all. That's why reshooting is such a waste.

## The check that makes it trustworthy

Cutting words out of your own mouth is surgery, so verify it like surgery. Every cut got checked word by word against what's actually IN the finished file, not what was supposed to be there. 1 video tried to leak the first word of an old ending past the cut. The check caught it before I ever saw it. If you can't verify the cut, you haven't made the cut.

## Run this prompt

Paste this into Claude (or any AI):

```
I have old content that's out of date on branding, but the teaching in it is still good. Help me plan a refresh with 0 re-recording.

My old content: [list it: videos, pages, PDFs, course lessons, with rough lengths]
What changed: [new brand name, new look, dropped offer, new website, whatever made the old stuff wrong]
What must NOT change: [usually: your voice, the teaching, anything customers already rely on]

Do this:
1. Sort every piece into 3 buckets: SKIN SWAP (visuals only), CUT (old pitches and dead names that should just be removed), and KEEP (untouched teaching).
2. For each piece, write the 1-line work order: what gets swapped, what gets cut, what stays.
3. Tell me what ingredients you'd need me to dig up 1 time (original audio, source files, the new brand rules) to do this without re-recording.
4. Design the verification step: how we prove, word by word, that no dead brand name or old pitch survived in the finished files.
5. Estimate the honest order of work, smallest piece first, so the first win lands today.
```
