# Numbered Drawers, And The Part Where You Look

Yo! It's RyMac. 2 things fall straight out of the desk idea, and these are
the parts that actually made me trust it.

## 1. The drawers are numbered

If the desk can only hold one folder at a time, then the folders have to go in
an order.

So they get numbers. `01_research`, `02_draft`, `03_edit`.

**Drawer 1 becomes drawer 2.** Research before you write. Write before you
edit. Which sounds obvious, and that is sort of the point. You already know
the order of your own work. Nobody ever made you write it down where the
machine could see it.

The number is not decoration. It is the instruction. An AI reading a folder
called `02_draft` next to a folder called `01_research` knows which one feeds
which without being told.

## 2. Before the folder goes back in the drawer, you look at it

This is the one.

Not because the AI is stupid. Because **it is your drawer.** You are the one
who knows if it is right.

That is the whole difference between this and turning a swarm of robots loose
for 20 minutes and getting handed something you now have to audit backwards to
figure out where it went wrong.

Here, every single step leaves a file sitting on your desk that you can open,
read, and fix before anything moves forward.

If you have been nervous about handing real work to AI, that is the part that
should relax you. **You never give up the wheel. You are just not typing as
much.**

## Why this beats "just give it better instructions"

A long instruction is a promise. A numbered folder is a place.

When the work lives in `01_` and the output lands in `02_`, you can see where
you are. When it all lives in one prompt, you find out it went wrong at the
end, when it is expensive.

## Run this prompt

Paste this into your AI along with this file:

```
Help me turn one repeating process in my business into numbered drawers.

The process: [NAME IT, e.g. "how I take a new client from inquiry to
onboarded" or "how I produce one piece of content"]
How I do it now, in whatever order it comes out: [BRAIN DUMP THE STEPS,
messy is fine]
The part I am most afraid of an AI getting wrong: [YOUR ANSWER]

Do this:
1. Turn my mess into 3 to 5 numbered stages, named 01_, 02_, 03_ style, in
   the order they actually have to happen. Use my words for the names.
2. For each stage write a one-line CONTEXT.md: what goes IN, what happens,
   what comes OUT. One line each, no essays.
3. Mark which stage is the review gate, where I have to look at the file
   before it moves on. Tell me exactly what I am checking for there.
4. Tell me which stage I could hand over entirely and never look at, and why
   that one is safe.

Do not invent stages I did not describe. If I skipped something obvious, ask
me about it instead of filling it in.
```

Make the folders with the names it gives you. Empty folders are fine. The
structure is the point.
