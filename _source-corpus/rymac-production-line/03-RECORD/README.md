# 03-RECORD

Stage 4. The approved script becomes a fold, the fold becomes the reader, and
you record from the reader.

- `04-VO-FOLD.md` is the script converted to reader lines. `001.` becomes
  `[001]`. Nothing else changes.
- `05-VO-READER.html` is generated from the fold. It is a full-screen
  slideshow. One slide at a time, you
  click through it while you record. It is a crude mock of the finished video,
  never a document with notes.
- The reader only ships on a PASS from its byte gate. No em dashes, no
  periods, 14 words a slide, 1 second holds, your declared skin.

The 1 law that protects your tape: while you record, NOTHING runs on your
machine. No uploads, no renders, no file copies. A starved capture corrupts
the recording and you find out after the render.

Skill that runs here: `rymac-make-a-reader-to-record-from`.
