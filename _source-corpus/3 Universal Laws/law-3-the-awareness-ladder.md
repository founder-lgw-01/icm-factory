# Law 3: The Right Message (The Awareness Ladder)

Yo! It's RyMac. This is the one nobody teaches, at least not the way I use it. It's also the fuel that makes Law 2 work.

## Only 3 to 5% are ready right now

Only 3 to 5% of any market is ready to buy right now. Everyone else, you burn the second you ask "ready to schedule?" or "it's X to get started, ready to go?" That's direct, most-aware messaging. You're talking to the far right of the ladder. For the other 95%, it lands like a stranger proposing marriage on the first date.

## The ladder (right to left)

Every buyer stands on one of 5 rungs. You meet them where they are:

- **Most Aware** (far right): they know you and the product. They want the price. "It's $3,000 for me to build your roof."
- **Product Aware:** they know it exists. They want a discount or a deal. "That $3,000 quote, I'll take 10% off if you act now."
- **Solution Aware:** they know solutions exist, not that yours is best. They want proof. "We have 305 5-star reviews on Google, go check them out."
- **Problem Aware:** they feel the pain, not the fix. They want the benefit and their fear named. "If you don't fix that leaky roof, your house molds and your kids get sick."
- **Unaware** (far left): they don't know they have a problem. They only move for a story or a secret. "Did you know if you have a 700 credit score, the banks will hand your LLC $50,000 off your personal credit?"

## The walk-back (this is the move)

Everybody assumes every lead is on the far right, so their first message is all price. Wrong. When the price message gets no answer, you don't say it louder. **You walk one rung to the left.** No answer on price? Next touch is the discount. Still nothing? Proof. Still quiet? Name the pain. All the way down to a story for the ones who never knew they were broken. Every unanswered message doesn't get louder, it gets EARLIER on the journey.

## How Law 3 powers Law 2

Here's the part that ties it together. 5 rungs = 5 completely different messages. Now write each rung 3 or 4 different ways. That's **12 to 24 real follow-ups that never once repeat themselves.** Run them across SMS, email, DMs, and a voice drop and it multiplies again. THAT is how you follow up 20 times and it feels like 20 conversations, not 20 nags. And because it takes weeks to walk all the way left, you catch people deep in the sequence right when their situation finally changes. The lead who ignored you in January answers the story in March, because now they feel the problem.

## Run this prompt

Paste this into your AI with this file:

```
The business + offer: [WHAT THEY SELL, AND THE PRICE OR STARTING POINT].
Their real proof: [reviews, results, case studies, guarantees they actually have].
The customer's deepest pain if they do nothing: [what goes wrong].

Using the ladder in this file, write me the full follow-up sequence, one
message per rung, walking right to left:
1. Most Aware: the direct price / book-a-call message.
2. Product Aware: a discount, deal, or reason to act now.
3. Solution Aware: the proof message (use their real proof).
4. Problem Aware: name the benefit and the fear, honestly.
5. Unaware: a story or a surprising secret that starts a conversation.

Then, for the 2 rungs most likely to land in this niche, give me 3 different
phrasings each, so the follow-up never repeats. Keep every message in this
business's voice, honest, and ending in a soft next step. No hype, no fake
scarcity.
```

The right message is the ammo that makes persistence work. This is the cornerstone.
