## Why this works at all

Your AI works off a desk.

The desk is what it can see when it starts. If the desk is empty, it guesses.
If the desk holds a card that says who you are, what you sell, and how you
sound, it works like someone who has been with you for a year.

`_config/my-line.md` is that card. The interview writes it. Every skill in this
kit reads it before it does anything.

You only open what you can see. Your AI only knows what you tell it.

## The interview

Your AI asks you these 9 questions, 1 at a time. It waits for each answer. It
never guesses an answer for you. Plain words are the right words.

1. What is your brand, and which website is its front door?
2. What is your niche, and who buys from you?
3. What is your YouTube channel?
4. Where does your blog live?
5. Where does your community live, if you have one?
6. Do you have a research and channel management tool? If you don't, the
   recommendation is vidIQ, and your AI will note it either way.
7. What should your videos look like? Give a direction in plain words, dark
   and bold, paper and ink, bright and clean. Or give your exact brand color
   codes if you know them. Either answer works.
8. What 3 lines end every video? Your sign-off, your promise, your name.
9. What is the 1 video you already know you should be making on repeat?

**This is why.** Right now every file in this kit runs on placeholder answers.
Every time you make a video, you would be correcting it by hand.

**This is what it does.** Your 9 answers become the card on the desk. The
scripts end on your close. The readers wear your colors, not mine. The blog
posts land on your site. You answer once. It holds.

## The 9 steps, so you know the shape

Gameplan. Script. Spec. Reader. Storyboard. 90 second render. Full render.
Package. Blog.

You do not need to memorize them. Your AI carries them, and it reads the list
out loud before it builds anything. The 1 rule you enforce as the owner: no
step gets skipped to save time. Every expensive day on this line came from a
skipped step, not a missing skill.
