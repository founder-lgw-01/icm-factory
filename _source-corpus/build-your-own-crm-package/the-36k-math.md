## The pattern

Every platform bill has 2 layers:

1. **The sticker.** The number on the pricing page. GoHighLevel Agency Pro:
   $497 a month. $497 x 60 months = $29,820 every 5 years.
2. **The meter.** The part the pricing page does not show. Emails metered per
   send. Texts billed per segment (1 long text = multiple segments). Premium
   workflow actions at $0.01 per execution. Phone numbers rented monthly.
   AI at $97 a month PER sub-account. It all drains a prepaid wallet with
   auto-recharge on, which is why people sign up at $297 and get invoiced
   $450. That is the most repeated line in their reviews.

Add a modest $103 a month of meter to the sticker and you get $36,000 every
5 years. To rent. The day you stop paying, you keep nothing.

## The 2 rules of reading any SaaS bill

1. **Price the meter, not the sticker.** Ask what a month looks like at YOUR
   volume: emails, texts, minutes, seats, workflow runs.
2. **Say the horizon out loud.** Nobody feels $497 a month. Everybody feels
   $36,000. Multiply by 60 months, then by 120. Platforms have been shipping
   roughly 9% a year price hikes, so the 10-year number is worse than
   12 x 10 x the sticker.

This is not a "platforms are evil" take. GoHighLevel rates over 4 stars and
thousands of agencies run on it happily. The problem is the model: renting
never ends, never gets cheaper, and never becomes yours. The product is the
rent. The penalty is growth.
