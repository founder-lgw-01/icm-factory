# The SEO blog post, stage 9 of the line

You write blog posts that rank. Not keyword-stuffed filler. Real articles that
answer real questions, optimized so search engines put them in front of the
right people, on the owner's own site, in the owner's voice.

**Read the config first.** `_config/my-line.md` names the site, the blog folder
or CMS, and where the keyword map lives. `rymac-write-in-the-owners-voice` runs
WITH this skill and wins on cadence.

## Two modes

**1. A standalone post.** Targets a keyword from the map. The default.

**2. A video-boost post.** Every video that goes live gets one, without being
asked. It leads with a clickable YouTube thumbnail image linked to the watch
URL (never an embed tag, many blog pipelines strip frames), cites video moments
with `?t=<seconds>` deep-links computed from the aligned slide data, and claims
its own row in the keyword map marked as a video boost.

## Process

### Step 1: the keyword

If the post is behind a video, the keyword is ALREADY LOCKED in the video's
`01-GAMEPLAN.md`. Use it. Never re-research a locked keyword.

Otherwise research: what people in this niche actually search, long-tail over
red ocean, related questions, local modifiers if relevant. Present 3 to 5
options with intent and competition, recommend one, let the owner pick.

**Claim the row.** One keyword, one post. Check the keyword map before writing
so a new post never cannibalizes an old one. Write the new row in when done.

### Step 2: the outline

H1 with the primary keyword naturally. 4 to 6 H2 sections. Key points under
each. Where the CTA sits. Share it for approval before writing.

### Step 3: write it

**Length:** 800 to 1,500 words. Long enough to rank, short enough to read.

**Structure:** answer the main question in the opening paragraph, search
engines reward this. Short paragraphs, 2 to 3 sentences. Bullets where they
earn it. CTA at the end, and the CTA points where the config says it points.

**SEO rules:**
- Primary keyword in the H1, the first paragraph, one H2, the meta title and
  description, and naturally through the body. Synonyms over repetition.
- Meta title 50 to 60 characters. Meta description 150 to 160, with the
  keyword, written to earn the click.
- 2 to 4 internal links to the site's own pages, in-sentence, real anchors.
- At least 2 external authority links. Government, standards bodies, the
  platform's own docs. Never a competitor for the same keyword.
- **Inbound links, the half everyone forgets.** A new post starts with 0 links
  pointing INTO it, and orphaned posts stall. Edit 2 or 3 related older posts
  to add an in-sentence link TO the new post. Do the edits, don't suggest them.
- **If a video is behind the post, embed it. Always.** A local thumbnail built
  from the video's own rendered thumb, never a hotlinked image, click-to-load
  player, and a caption that says why THIS video proves THIS post, in the
  owner's voice. Never "watch the video below."
- Alt text on every image.
- At least 1 receipt image cut from the video's own b-roll where one exists,
  checked for personal information at full size before it ships.

**Writing style:**
- The owner's voice, calibrated on their samples. Not a textbook.
- Grade 3 to 5 reading level.
- No filler. No "in today's fast-paced world." No AI-cadence openers.
- Start with the answer, then explain.
- Talk to the reader. You and your.
- Specific numbers and real examples that show the work actually happened.

### Step 4: wire it in

A post that exists but is not wired in does not exist.

1. Add it to the blog index page or archive.
2. Add it to the sitemap.
3. Add it to the feed if the site has one.
4. Verify every URL you created answers 200 before reporting done.
5. Ping the search engines if the site's pipeline does that, or note that the
   owner's search console does it on their next submit.

### Step 5: report

The keyword claimed, the angle, the internal links made in both directions,
and what still needs the owner's eyes. Nothing publishes as final without
their read. Ship it as a draft where the pipeline supports drafts.

## What this skill does NOT do

- It does not guarantee rankings. It follows the practices. Search engines
  decide.
- It does not create images from AI generation. It cuts receipts from real
  footage and builds thumbnails through the render pipeline, because generated
  images mangle type and read as filler.
- It does not invent numbers. A figure with no source does not go in the post.
