# Term Sheet

Every term from the video, in plain language. No question here is too basic.

**The 3 Laws** · The 3 things every business breaks: Speed, Persistence, and the Right Message. Fix a broken one with AI and that's your offer.

**The leak** · A broken law. A business bleeding customers it already paid to get. Finding the leak is finding what to sell.

**The sticker** · The niche-specific name for the same 3 laws. Home service = "landed jobs," lawyers = "landing cases," dentists = "landing new patients." Same laws, different word for the customer.

**Speed to lead** · Law 1. How fast you answer a new lead. Under 5 minutes is the standard; the average business takes over 40 hours.

**The 5-minute window** · You're 100x more likely to reach a lead at 5 minutes than at 30, and 78% buy from whoever answers first. Miss it and the lead is cold.

**Persistence / follow-up cadence** · Law 2. The series of touches after the first no. 80% of sales happen on touches 5 through 12; most people quit before 5.

**Touches 5 to 12** · The money zone. Where 80% of sales close and 92% of competitors have already quit.

**The right message** · Law 3. Saying the thing that matches where the buyer actually is, instead of one message for everyone.

**The awareness ladder** · The 5 rungs a buyer stands on, from Unaware (far left) to Most Aware (far right). Borrowed from Eugene Schwartz's 5 stages of awareness.

**The 5 rungs** · Unaware (stories & secrets) → Problem Aware (benefits & anxieties) → Solution Aware (claims & proof) → Product Aware (discounts & deals) → Most Aware (product & price).

**Direct vs indirect messaging** · Direct = right side (price, book a call), for the ready buyer. Indirect = left side (stories, pain), for the ones who aren't ready yet.

**The walk-back** · The move. When a message gets no answer, you don't say it louder, you walk one rung LEFT (price → deal → proof → pain → story). Every unanswered message gets EARLIER on the journey.

**"Law 3 powers Law 2"** · The cornerstone idea. 5 rungs, each written 3 to 4 ways, across SMS/email/DM/voice = 12 to 24 follow-ups that never repeat. That's how you follow up 20 times without being a nag.

**The 3% / the 95%** · Only 3 to 5% of a market is ready to buy right now. Ask "ready to schedule?" and you burn the other 95%.

**Voice drop** · A pre-recorded voicemail dropped to a lead's phone without ringing it. One of the follow-up channels.

**The fix is the product** · You don't sell a template. You find the broken law and sell the fix. The leak is the reason they buy.
