# Member Builds: the room record

This file is the community's trophy wall. When you build something with the
files in this package and post it in the community, you go in here, name
kept. The record grows with every drop.

Why it exists: seeing a name you recognize next to a real build beats any
testimonial. This room ships and this room checks receipts.

## What counts

You do not need a finished funnel. Any of these go in the record:

- You scaffolded a folder system and it changed how your AI behaves
- You ran the research prompt and it told you something about your market you did not know
- You collapsed a multi-agent setup into 1 agent and a folder
- You caught something wrong in the video or in these files
- You wrote your first 3 emails and somebody actually replied

That last 1 is the one worth posting. Somebody replying to email 1 is the
whole point of the sequence.
