# Term Sheet

Every term from the video, in plain language. No question in the community
is too basic; this file exists so you never have to ask "wait, what's a
repo?"

**Claude Code** · A program that runs in a terminal and writes code when you
type English at it. The AI contractor from the video.

**Terminal** · The black text window programmers use. Scary looking, is
actually just a place where you type. Claude Code lives there.

**Repo (repository)** · A project folder that git tracks. Your website is
one repo. It lives in YOUR GitHub account.

**Git** · The tool that saves versions of your code. Think "track changes"
for entire projects.

**git push** · The command that sends your saved code up to GitHub. In this
system, pushing is also what puts your site live (Vercel watches for it).

**GitHub** · The website where repos live, under your account. Free. Rule 1
of ownership: the code lives in YOUR account.

**Vercel** · The service that takes your repo and puts it on the internet.
Free tier. You connect it to GitHub once; after that, every push deploys.

**Deploy** · Putting your site live on the internet. "It deployed" = it is
live at your URL.

**Domain** · Your address on the internet (yourname.com). About $12 a year.
You point it at Vercel.

**Free tier** · The genuinely free level of a service. The video's point:
the free tiers of GitHub, Vercel, Cloudflare, and Firebase are so large a
small business never crosses them.

**CRM** · Customer relationship management software. The thing that holds
your contacts, deals, pipeline, and calendar. The thing I built instead of
renting.

**Sticker vs the meter** · The sticker is the advertised monthly price. The
meter is the usage billing on top (per email, per text segment, per workflow
run, per AI seat). Real bills = sticker + meter.

**The wallet drain** · Prepaid usage credits with auto-recharge on. Why a
$297 signup becomes a $450 invoice.

**Growth tax** · When the bill grows because YOU grew: more contacts, more
seats, more sends, higher tier. The penalty is growth.

**Skill** · A folder you drop into Claude Code that teaches it a method.
Say "build me a landing page" and it builds it that way every time. Mine
are free at github.com/oathdriven/rymac-skills.

**Hero (the fold)** · The top screen of a page, what you see before
scrolling. Where the headline lives.

**Callout** · The pre-headline naming exactly who the page is for.

**Support** · The sub-headline or bullets under the headline that kill the
reader's top 3 pains.

**VSL** · Video sales letter. The video on a sales page that does the
selling.

**CTA** · Call to action. The one thing you ask the visitor to do.

**The hidden row** · The last line of the calculator, revealed after the
prospect has agreed to every number above it. See the Nobody Argues file.

**The analyzer** · The calculator on allodra.ai that runs YOUR 10-year rent
math. 1 scroll down.
