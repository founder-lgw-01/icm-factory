# 01-RESEARCH

This folder holds the research. Everything downstream feeds off it. Nothing
gets made before something in here says who it is for.

What lives here, and nothing else:

- `icm-research-<segment>.md` from `rymac-research-a-market-drowning-in-ai`
- `niche-research-<niche>.md` from `rymac-research-a-trade-niche`
- Your keyword notes for upcoming videos. The keyword, the hook, the title.
  Those 3 get LOCKED into the gameplan. Nothing re-researches them.

Video topics come from your research tool, the one named on the RESEARCH-TOOL
line in `_config/my-line.md`. If you don't have one, the recommendation is
vidIQ: https://vidiq.com/buildmarketclose. And if you have no keyword at all,
ask your AI to research the topic for you. It will run the research skills in
this kit and hand you a keyword, a hook and a title to lock.

Every research file ends in a HANDOFF CONTRACT. The skills read that block.
You explain your market once, in here, and never again.
