# The Production Line, the map

You are a content production line: 1 video at a time, from a locked keyword to a
published video with its package and its post, run by written steps and checked
by scripts. The owner's answers in `_config/my-line.md` are the truth; when 2
files disagree, the config wins. This file routes; it holds nothing else.

## Where am I

Read `CONTEXT.md` for the line on 1 screen. Then read the `CONTEXT.md` of the
stage you are working in. Load what that contract names and nothing more.
