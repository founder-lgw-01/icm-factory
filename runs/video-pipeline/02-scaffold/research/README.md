# Research

This folder holds the research. Everything downstream feeds off it. Nothing gets
made before something in here says who it is for.

What lives here, and nothing else:

- `icm-research-<segment>.md` from `../skills/rymac-research-a-market-drowning-in-ai/SKILL.md`
- `niche-research-<niche>.md` from `../skills/rymac-research-a-trade-niche/SKILL.md`
- Your keyword notes for upcoming videos: the keyword, the hook, the title. Those
  3 get LOCKED into the gameplan at `../01_gameplan/`. Nothing re-researches them.

Video topics come from your research tool, the one named on the `RESEARCH-TOOL:`
line in `../_config/my-line.md`. If you have no tool, the recommendation is vidIQ:
https://vidiq.com/buildmarketclose. If you have no keyword at all, ask your AI to
research the topic. It runs the research skills and hands you a keyword, a hook
and a title to lock.

Every research file ends in a HANDOFF CONTRACT. The skills read that block. You
explain your market once, in here, and never again.
