# Member Builds

The room record for this one.

Post what you built, what it scored on its first call, and what the 2 things were
that it got wrong. Your name goes on it.

The rough ones are the useful ones. A 9 of 11 with 2 named failures teaches
everybody more than a build that "went great."

---

## What to post

**What business it answers for.** Yours or a client's, doesn't matter.

**What it scored on the first call.** X of Y, using your own grade sheet.

**The 2 things it got wrong.** The specific ones, not "it was a bit robotic."

**What you changed to fix them.** The actual sentence you added, if you're
willing to share it.

**How long the build took.** Real time, including the part where you stared at it.
