# Law 2: Persistence (The Money Is in Touches 5 to 12)

Yo! It's RyMac. Law 2 is where most of the money is hiding, and almost nobody walks in to get it.

## The numbers

The first no is not a no. It's the starting line.

- **80%** of sales happen between the 5th and 12th follow-up.
- But **92%** of salespeople quit before that 5th touch.
- **44%** quit after just ONE.

So the money is sitting in follow-ups 5 through 12, and almost nobody goes there. Set a business up with at least 5 follow-ups and you put them ahead of 92% of their competition. Trust me, business owners love being ahead of their competition.

## The fix (and the trap)

Build the engine that follows up forever and never gets tired. AI can send touch number 11 at 7am with the same energy as touch number 1.

But here's where every automation guy face-plants: what do you SAY 12 times without turning into a pest? Send "just following up" 12 times and you're not persistent, you're annoying.

The answer is Law 3. Persistence is the engine; the awareness ladder (Law 3) is the fuel that gives you 12 to 24 messages that never repeat. Read the Law 3 file next; this file is the cadence, that one is what goes in it.

Paste this into your AI with this file:

```
The business: [WHAT THEY SELL].

[nothing / one call / a couple emails].

Using Law 2 in this file:
1. Build me a follow-up cadence of at least 12 touches over 2 to 4 weeks:
   day, channel, and the JOB of each touch (not the words yet, the ladder
   file handles words). Front-load touches 1 to 5, then space out 6 to 12.
2. Mark exactly where touches 5 through 12 sit, since that's where 80% of the
   money is, so the owner sees it.
3. Tell me the one rule that keeps this from feeling like spam.
4. Give me the plain-English spec: what starts the sequence, what pauses it
   (a reply), and what ends it (booked or a hard no).
```
