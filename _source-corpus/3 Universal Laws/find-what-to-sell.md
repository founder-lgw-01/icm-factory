# Find What to Sell (The 3 Laws Are Your Offer)

Yo! It's RyMac. If you're stuck on "what do I even sell," this is the file. You don't need a shiny new product. You don't need to spin up an AI website template and spam 50 owners. Every business you'll ever look at already has a leak. Your job is to find it and plug it with AI. The fix is the product. The leak is the reason they buy.

## The 3 Laws every business breaks

Without knowing one thing about a business, I can tell you they're breaking at least one of these. 9 out of 10, they're breaking all 3:

1. **Speed.** They don't answer new leads fast. A lead is 100x more likely to connect at 5 minutes than at 30, and the average business takes over 40 hours.
2. **Persistence.** They don't follow up. 80% of sales happen between the 5th and 12th touch, and 92% of people quit before the 5th.
3. **The right message.** They say the same thing to everyone. Only 3 to 5% of a market is ready to buy right now, so one "ready to schedule?" burns the other 95%.

Break one and the business bleeds customers it already paid to get. That's lost revenue sitting on the table, and that's your opening.

## Same 3 laws, different sticker

This is not niche specific. It's 3 universal laws. It's how I've gone into dozens of niches and just put a different sticker on the same thing:

- "Close 4x more estimates in 48 hours using the 3 Laws of Landed Jobs."
- "Get 2x more patients in 7 days using the 3 Laws of Landing New Patients."
- "Increase new clients 50% in 30 days using the 3 Laws of Landing Cases."

Same laws. Different word for the customer. That's your USP, your headlines, and your website, handed to you.

## Run this prompt

Paste this into your AI with this file:

```
The business I want to help: [WHAT THEY DO + WHO THEY SERVE, e.g. "a roofing
company that sells roof replacements to homeowners"].

What that business calls a new customer: [jobs / patients / cases / clients / bookings].

Using the 3 Laws in this file:
1. Diagnose this business against all 3 laws (Speed, Persistence, Right
   Message). For each, tell me the likely leak and what it's probably costing
   them in plain language.
2. Pick the ONE law that is the biggest, most obvious leak for this niche and
   explain why it's the wedge.
3. Write me an offer around fixing that leak with AI: a one-line promise, the
   mechanism (what the AI actually does), and who it's for.
4. Write 3 USP / headline options in the format "[Outcome] in [timeframe]
   using the 3 Laws of [what this niche calls landing a customer]."
5. Keep it honest. No fake guarantees. The fix is real work; the offer just
   names the leak and the outcome.
```

That's the whole game: find the broken law, fix it with AI, and that's what you sell.
