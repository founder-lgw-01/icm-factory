# The 4 shapes

Every output from this skill takes 1 of these 4 forms. Pick the mode, follow the
shape, then run the checks in `SKILL.md`.

---

## Shape 1. Teach a file

For a START-HERE, a SETUP sheet, a README, a community lesson, or the teaching
section of a blog post.

```
# <plain title, 2 or 3 words>

<1 line: how long this takes and what happens at the end>

## What this actually is
<the thing, stripped. "This is a filing system for your business.">
<then kill the expectation of complexity, because people go hunting for it>

## What you are about to SEE
<name every file and folder they will look at, and say which ones are
written for THEM and which are written for their AI. Nobody tells them,
and it is the first thing that trips them up.>

## What you are about to DO
<the whole job in 4 lines or fewer, before any detail. So they know the
size of it before they start.>

## Why this works at all
<the desk. Always the desk. metaphors.md entry 1.>
<then the 3 pieces: a map, a desk, a filing cabinet.>

## The steps
<each one a TEACHING UNIT. The next step is / This is why / This is what
it does. Never a bare instruction.>

## What to do now
<1 file to open next, or 1 thing to go do. Never 2 options.>

<your community link>
Semper Fi,
<your name>
<your site>
```

**Rules for this shape**

- Every step is a teaching unit. No exceptions, including the easy ones.
- Give the literal click, the literal key, the literal file name. He is not a
  coder and neither are they.
- If a step can fail, put 1 verification line under it. "You should now see 4
  folders." Never a branch tree of what ifs.
- Never a menu. If there are 2 ways, pick the better one and give only that. The
  1 exception already in the kit is the 2 doors, and even that ends with "pick
  door 1 unless you already have door 2."

## Shape 2. Answer 1 question

For a community comment, a DM, a YouTube reply, or a question on a call. Output is a
reply he can paste. Nothing else.

```
<the picture first, 1 or 2 sentences, straight from metaphors.md>

<the plain meaning, no jargon>

<the sandwich, if they used a fancy word: "You may hear that called X.
It simply means Y.">

<1 thing to go do, or 1 thing to go look at>
```

**Rules for this shape**

- No preamble. No "great question", no "that is a actually common one".
- Answer the question that was asked. Do not teach the whole system at them.
- Write 4 short paragraphs at most. This is a comment box and it is not a page.
- If they are frustrated, the first move is always to say this. You are not bad
  at this.
- Never end on a pitch. End on the thing to go do.

## Shape 3. Walk me through

For somebody doing the kit right now. This is a conversation, not a document.

**1 teaching unit per turn. Then stop and wait.**

```
Step <n> of <total>. <what this step is called>

The next step is <the literal action, with the click or the file name>.

This is why. <the problem it solves in their business>

This is what it does. <what changes once it is done>

You should now see <the thing that proves it worked>.

Tell me when that is done and I will give you the next one.
```

**Rules for this shape**

- Never dump 8 steps at once. That is the failure this mode exists to fix.
- Say which number they are on and how many are left. People need the end in
  sight.
- If they say it did not work, do not repeat the step. Ask what they see on their
  screen, then fix from there.
- If they went quiet 2 steps back, the step was too big. Split it.

## Shape 4. Jargon sweep

For a page, post, or script that is already written. Output is a list of swaps,
not a rewritten file, unless he asks for the rewrite.

```
<file path>

Line <n>  "<the sentence as written>"
          → "<the sentence with the sandwich in it>"
          why: <which law it broke>
```

**Rules for this shape**

- Never delete a fancy word. Sandwich it instead. Swapping the word is the job.
- Quote the real line off disk, with its real line number. Not a paraphrase.
- Flag the 3 outright bans separately: a token saving percentage, "sold" as the
  verb, and anything that jabs at Jake.
- Do not touch proper nouns. Interpretable Context Methodology, CLAUDE.md,
  Context.md, product names.
- Count them at the end. "31 swaps across 4 files" is the useful line.
- He never rewrites a post himself. This is a swap job, never a writing job.
