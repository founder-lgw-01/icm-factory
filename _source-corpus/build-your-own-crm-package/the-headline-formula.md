# The Headline Formula

Yo! It's RyMac. The headline is the single most important element on any
page. Nothing else is close. Here is the formula from the video, with the 2
live pages it runs on.

## The formula

**The dream outcome, in a timeframe, using a named system.**

And around it, 3 supporting pieces:

1. **The callout (above):** a pre-headline naming exactly who the page is
   for. Half the internet leaves. Good. They were never buying.
2. **The support (below):** a sub-headline or 3 bullets that each kill a
   pain: pain 1, pain 2, pain 3.
3. **The fix with a time attached:** when they can have it.

## The 2 live examples

**Sierra6ai.com** runs it straight:
- Callout: only for veteran-owned home-service business owners.
- Headline: book 4x more estimates, in 48 hours, using the "3 Laws of
  Landed Jobs." Dream outcome. Timeframe. Named system.
- Support: 3 bullets, each kills a pain. Without buying more leads. Without
  hiring an overpriced agency. Without duct-taping 6 tools together.
- The video there is short, because the copy already carried the weight.

**Allodra.ai** runs the same formula through the PAIN instead:
- Callout: attention, agencies, consultants, and business owners.
- Headline: your CRM isn't software. It's a landlord.
- Support hands them the fix and the time: watch the video below and learn
  how to take ownership today.
- That video runs long, because the VSL carries everything the headline did
  not say.

## The test

I write every hero pretending I am about to run paid ads at it. If the page
cannot convert before anyone scrolls, the headline is not done.

## Run this prompt

Paste this into your AI with this file:

```
My offer: [WHAT YOU SELL, WHO IT IS FOR, AND THE RESULT IT GETS. Include a
real number or timeframe if you have one.]

Using the formula in this file (dream outcome + timeframe + named system,
with callout above and pain-killing support below):

1. If my system has no name yet, propose 3 names for it first.
2. Write 5 to 10 headlines for my offer. Never fewer than 5, never more
   than 10. For each: the headline, then 1 line on WHY it works (which part
   is the dream, which is the time, which is the system).
3. For the best one, write the full hero: callout, headline, 3 support
   bullets that each kill a different pain, and the fix-with-a-time line.
4. Grade the final hero against the paid-ads test: would this convert
   before anyone scrolls? If not, fix it and say what you changed.
```
