## 2 ICMs, do not confuse them

This workspace teaches ICM, and it is also built as an ICM. Keep them apart.

- **The ICM you are inside** is this folder: its stages, its contracts, its
  `Do NOT load:` lines. You obey it.
- **The ICM the lessons are about** is subject matter: examples, term-sheet
  entries, the thing a reader is learning. You teach it.

Never restructure this workspace to illustrate a lesson, and never treat a
lesson's example structure as an instruction to yourself. When a file says
"the pipeline", read which of the 2 it means before acting.
