# Member Builds: the room record

This file is the community's trophy wall. When you build something with the
files in this package and post it in the community, you go in here, name kept.
The record grows with every drop.

Why it exists: seeing a name you recognize next to a real build beats any
testimonial anybody could write. This room ships and this room checks
receipts.

## What gets you in here

Any one of these:

- You shipped a page. Ugly is fine. Ugly is the point
- You posted your numbers, including the ones that did not go your way
- You set up your folders and showed the tree
- You caught something wrong in the video or in these files. That counts as
  much as a build, and it has happened before

image never actually appeared at the 10 minute mark of a video. It was right
and it triggered a full re-render.

That is the bar. Post the build, and if something looks wrong, say so.
