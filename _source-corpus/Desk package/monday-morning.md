# Monday Morning

Yo! It's RyMac. This is the do-it-now file. Takes about 10 minutes and costs
nothing.

If you got handed "figure out our AI," or you are just trying to work out AI
for your own business, start here and skip the rest until this is done.

## The 10 minutes

**1. Open the folder you already work in.** The real one, where your stuff
already lives. Not a fresh demo folder. The messy one.

**2. Make one file in it. Call it `CLAUDE.md`.** It is a text file. That is
all it is. Notepad makes one. So does anything else.

**3. Write 3 things in it:**

- What this folder is for, in one sentence.
- Where things live, as a short list.
- What you never want touched.

That is it. That is a context layer. You just did the thing everybody is
making infographics about.

Now when you point an AI at that folder, it starts by reading your card
instead of guessing. And that difference is not subtle.

## Then stop

Do not design the rest of the system today. That is the mistake I made.

**Only when something annoys you, add the next piece.**

You keep re-explaining your brand voice? Make a file for the brand voice, and
put one line on the card saying where it is. You keep re-explaining your
pricing? Same move.

That is how this actually gets built. Not by designing a system on day 1. By
fixing whatever annoyed you today and writing down where you put it.

## You are not behind

You do not need to be technical for a second of this. It is folders and a
text file.

If you have ever organized a shared drive so your coworkers could find things,
you have already done the hard part. You just did it for people instead of
for a machine.

I am 50 days in, I cannot write a line of code, and I have broken most of
this at least once.

## Run this prompt

Paste this into your AI along with this file:

```
Walk me through making my first card, right now, for a folder I actually use.

First ask me these 3 questions ONE AT A TIME, and wait for my answer before
asking the next:
1. What is this folder for? One sentence, in your own words.
2. What is in it? Rough list, no order needed.
3. What in here should never be changed without you asking me first?

After my third answer, write the finished CLAUDE.md. Rules:
- Under 40 lines.
- Use my words, not consulting language.
- Every line has to be something you would actually act on.
- End it with a short "when in doubt, ask" line.

Then give me ONE thing to add next week, and tell me what specific annoyance
would be the signal that it is time to add it.
```

Save it as `CLAUDE.md`. That is Monday done.

There is a desk. It has a size limit. Give it a map instead of a pile.
